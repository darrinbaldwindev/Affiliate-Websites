# UK Affiliate Opportunity Ranking

**Workstream:** United Kingdom only  
**Status:** Research / candidate ranking  
**Updated:** 2026-09-03  
**Evidence rule:** commercial rates and terms are volatile and must be re-verified before publication.

## Purpose

Identify the UK affiliate programmes most suitable for the Affiliate Websites discovery, comparison and recommendation engine. Ranking is based on commercial potential, consumer intent, catalogue/content depth, technical/data suitability, trust and programme constraints rather than headline commission alone.

## Tier A — investigate first

### Currys
- Category: technology, appliances, gaming
- Strong fit for: product comparison, buying guides, deal discovery
- Current evidence: Awin lists 10,000+ products and programme commission groups that distinguish editorial/content from comparison and other partner types.
- Architecture note: product/feed data should be treated as volatile API data, not hard-coded theme content.
- Verification: required before launch.

### SharkNinja
- Category: home appliances, cleaning, cooking, beauty
- Strong fit for: high-intent product reviews and comparisons
- Current evidence: official UK affiliate page says the programme is powered by Awin, accepts publishers/influencers/content creators, offers competitive commissions and promotional opportunities, and requires programme approval.
- Architecture note: candidate for product-feed driven modules once feed access is confirmed.

### Alibaba UK
- Category: broad ecommerce / long-tail products
- Strong fit for: breadth and long-tail discovery
- Current evidence: current Awin profile advertises a large product catalogue, 60-day attribution and a new-buyer commission structure; rates are explicitly subject to programme terms and can change.
- Risk: trust, quality variance, returns and merchant/product filtering make governance especially important.

### Broadband comparison vertical
**Candidates:** The One Broadband, BT, Lightning Fibre and additional verified UK providers.
- Strong fit for: comparison pages because commissions can be CPA/fixed-value rather than low product percentages.
- The One Broadband current Awin profile confirms a 30-day attribution period and full-fibre tariffs at 150Mbps, 900Mbps and 2.2Gbps.
- This vertical requires careful availability/address qualification and current offer verification.

### Amazon UK
- Category: broad ecommerce
- Strong fit for: long-tail product coverage and fallback merchant coverage.
- Current official UK commission table shows category-dependent rates; examples include 6% Amazon Fashion, 5% Home/Books/Kitchen, 4% Beauty/Luggage/Sports & Fitness, 2.5% Appliances/Fire TV/Mobile Electronics, and 1% Grocery/Video Games, with other categories at 3%.
- Current operating-agreement changes effective 14 April 2026 add qualification and advertising restrictions, so compliance rules must be represented as programme metadata.

## Ranking model

Score every programme 0–5 on:

1. Expected revenue per qualified visitor
2. Purchase/lead intent
3. Average order value or CPA potential
4. Search demand and content depth
5. Catalogue breadth
6. Feed/API availability
7. Country-specific availability
8. Brand trust
9. Conversion evidence
10. Programme stability
11. Restrictions / attribution risk
12. Returns, cancellations or validation risk
13. Regulatory/compliance exposure
14. Comparison/recommendation suitability
15. Ability to create useful non-commercial editorial content

Do not publish a programme merely because it has a high commission rate.

## UK launch recommendation

The first commercial vertical slice should prioritise:

1. Currys — product comparison
2. SharkNinja — high-value appliance/product comparison
3. Amazon UK — long-tail coverage
4. Broadband — provider comparison with current eligibility/offer verification
5. Beauty — Boots / LOOKFANTASTIC / Sephora after programme verification
6. Travel — Booking.com / Viator / TUI after programme verification

This creates multiple commercial models: product percentage, high-AOV products, marketplace breadth, fixed CPA/lead generation, repeat-purchase categories and travel transactions.

## Data governance

All rates, cookie durations, product prices, stock, promotions, programme acceptance, eligibility and destination URLs are volatile. Store source, source type, observed date, effective date where known, verification status, confidence, freshness and change history. Do not hard-code volatile commercial facts into WordPress theme templates.
