SNDYS is deliberately held back because the network directory headline and programme description disagree on the commission rate.

## Publication gate

No candidate becomes PUBLISHABLE until the following are individually verified:

1. current Australian consumer availability;
2. current programme eligibility/approval;
3. exact commission calculation and exclusions;
4. attribution/tracking rules;
5. shipping and returns;
6. product/category suitability;
7. current merchant terms;
8. evidence timestamp.

Do not use commission rate alone to rank merchants or claim that a merchant is "best".
