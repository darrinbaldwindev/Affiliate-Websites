# USA Affiliate Application Pack

**Workstream:** USA Affiliate Website  
**Status:** PREPARATION ONLY — no application/approval implied  
**Updated:** 2026-09-15

## Purpose

Reusable, non-sensitive publisher application material for the US affiliate workstream. Private identity, tax, payment, credential, contract-acceptance and account-approval fields are intentionally excluded or left unresolved.

## Core publisher profile

### Positioning
A US-focused consumer decision-support website helping people compare legitimate ways to earn rewards or compensation from surveys, paid research, focus groups, interviews, UX/app testing, product testing, rewards platforms and related participation opportunities.

### Editorial promise
Recommendations are consumer-first. Programs are evaluated separately for consumer value and publisher monetisation. Referral relationships are not presented as publisher affiliate relationships. Earnings, eligibility, availability and commercial terms are not guaranteed and are published only when supported by current evidence.

### Audience
US consumers seeking legitimate paid-participation and rewards opportunities, plus a secondary professional audience of UX researchers, product managers, designers, research operations teams, agencies and educators evaluating research/recruitment tools.

### Content model
- evidence-backed program profiles;
- category and opportunity guides;
- comparisons and alternatives;
- eligibility, time, reward and privacy explanations;
- paid-research and UX-testing guides;
- professional research-tool comparisons;
- transparent affiliate disclosures.

### Intended acquisition methods
- organic search / SEO;
- answer-engine / AI discovery optimisation;
- editorial internal linking;
- direct/repeat visits;
- owned email/social only where subsequently implemented and compliant.

Do not claim paid search, incentivised traffic, coupon traffic, browser extensions, audience arbitrage, trademark bidding or other acquisition methods unless separately approved and actually implemented.

## Compliance statement

The project is designed to:
- disclose affiliate relationships clearly;
- distinguish publisher affiliate, participant/member referral, B2B referral and UNKNOWN relationships;
- avoid misleading or guaranteed-income claims;
- avoid publishing stale commission claims as current;
- retain evidence and verification dates for material commercial claims;
- keep raw tracking URLs out of editorial content;
- route monetised outbound clicks only through approved controlled destinations;
- fail closed when publisher evidence, eligibility, freshness, disclosure or destination approval is missing;
- give elevated privacy treatment to passive behavioural research, identity verification and sensitive categories.

## Standard disclosure copy

> Some links may be affiliate links. If you use one and complete the qualifying action, we may receive compensation. This does not increase what you pay or guarantee that you will qualify for, receive, or earn any particular reward. We evaluate consumer usefulness separately from affiliate compensation.

Program-specific disclosures must be used when a network or merchant requires different wording.

## Owner-input checklist

Only these unresolved owner/private fields block actual applications:

- legal applicant/entity and whether applications use an individual or company;
- actual tax residency and business-registration jurisdiction;
- primary publisher/site URL to submit;
- network contact email and phone;
- payment destination/details where required;
- tax identifiers/forms requested by each network;
- explicit acceptance of each network/program agreement;
- completion of identity/media-property verification when requested.

These values must not be inferred from user location, repository ownership, website country, or prior conversation.

## Destination gate

For every program:

`application prepared → owner/private fields completed → network/publisher approved → merchant/program accepted → private/current terms captured → controlled destination created → CTA governance revalidated → eligible for activation`

Until that chain is complete, `commercial.destination_id` remains absent/null and the monetised CTA remains fail-closed.

## Maintenance

Before submitting any application, revalidate the current program/network application page and promotion restrictions. Store only non-sensitive application narrative in the repository; do not commit credentials, tax IDs, identity documents, payment details or private tracking URLs.
