The merchant also documents optional Checkout+ return-shipping/insurance coverage.

**State:** VERIFIED PROGRAM SIGNAL / FINAL PUBLISHABILITY CHECK REQUIRED.

## Publication decision

No candidate is promoted to unconditional **PUBLISHABLE** from these public checks alone. The site should use **VERIFIED** only where the programme facts are explicitly qualified and current, and should keep final programme acceptance/eligibility and account-specific terms as a pre-publication gate.

SNDYS remains **HOLD** because of the previously identified commission-rate conflict.

## Marketing implication

The strongest immediate content architecture is not a generic “best affiliate programmes” list. Build useful AU buyer-intent content around category/merchant comparisons, with commission treated as internal economics rather than the primary consumer-facing selection criterion.
