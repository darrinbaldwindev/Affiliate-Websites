## Hard governance rules

- Cheapest price does not equal recommended merchant.
- A marketplace offer must not inherit the marketplace brand's trust score as proof of the underlying seller/key provenance.
- An affiliate commission must never influence the factual licence classification shown to users.
- No raw affiliate tracking URL belongs in editorial content.
- Price, stock, coupon, commission and licence availability are volatile and require freshness metadata.
- Windows/Office offers require licence-type evidence (for example retail/OEM/volume/other where known) and must not be represented as equivalent merely because product names match.
- UNKNOWN provenance remains UNKNOWN.
- No production publication is authorised by this batch.
