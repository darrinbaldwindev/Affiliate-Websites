## Network-level evidence

Commission Factory states that joining as an affiliate has no fee. Its application process requires at least one traffic source and publisher review; therefore network membership and advertiser-programme acceptance are separate gates.

## Marketing interpretation

The strongest content-led candidates remain Snooza and Kurgo because their propositions support differentiated editorial clusters rather than generic store/category pages. Petzyo is commercially interesting but must distinguish new-customer from returning-customer economics. Pet Circle has broad utility but its detailed commission structure should be represented rather than relying on the directory headline.

## Publication blockers

No candidate is promoted to **PUBLISHABLE** solely from directory evidence. Before publication, confirm from the live publisher account/program terms:

1. Affiliate-Websites publisher approval for the advertiser.
2. Current commission rules and exclusions.
3. Allowed promotional methods and prohibited traffic sources.
4. Tracking/cookie and attribution rules.
5. Product/feed availability and freshness.
6. Current merchant shipping/returns for the exact products being compared.
7. Evidence supporting any substantive product or safety/wellness claim.

## Evidence classification

- **VERIFIED:** current programme listing exists and exposes the stated programme signal.
- **UNKNOWN:** Affiliate-Websites account-level acceptance and advertiser-specific approval status.
- **UNKNOWN:** realised conversion, revenue, earnings, demand and WTP.
- **RECOMMENDATION:** prioritise Snooza and Kurgo for the first account-level verification pass.
