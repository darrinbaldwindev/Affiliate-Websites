## Product/content evidence

Snooza's current official catalogue contains premium dog beds and accessories, including several products above AUD $100 and $200, creating credible editorial comparison opportunities. Kurgo's official catalogue strongly supports dog travel/outdoor/safety content, but its direct-store shipping/returns page is a US storefront policy and must not be repurposed as Australian policy. Petzyo's official site confirms Australian-made/owned dog food, recurring meal plans and current Australian customer servicing.

## Marketing interpretation

1. **Petzyo** now has the cleanest merchant-owned evidence that an affiliate programme exists and accepts partner applications.
2. **Kurgo** has clear merchant-owned affiliate evidence and strong content fit, but AU fulfilment/attribution requires separate verification.
3. **Snooza** has strong Australian merchant/product evidence, but the affiliate relationship remains a network-level signal until independently confirmed from the merchant or live publisher account.
4. **Pet Circle** remains high-utility, but current programme-rate presentation must follow the detailed programme terms.

## Publication blockers

No candidate is promoted to **PUBLISHABLE** solely from these findings. Before publication, confirm from the live publisher account/program terms:

1. Affiliate-Websites publisher approval for the advertiser.
2. Current commission rules and exclusions.
3. Allowed promotional methods and prohibited traffic sources.
4. Tracking/cookie and attribution rules.
5. Product/feed availability and freshness.
6. Current merchant shipping/returns for the exact products being compared.
7. Evidence supporting any substantive product, safety or wellness claim.
