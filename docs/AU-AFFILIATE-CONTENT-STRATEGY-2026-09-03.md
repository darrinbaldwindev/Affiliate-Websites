# Australia Affiliate — Content Strategy Update — 2026-09-03

**Status:** STRATEGY / READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION REVIEW

## Strategic decision

Do not build the initial site around a generic list of affiliate programmes. Build around consumer decisions and comparisons, then use verified affiliate relationships as the commercial path.

## Initial content pillars

1. **Compare before you buy** — side-by-side merchant/product comparisons with price, delivery, returns, key differences and evidence freshness.
2. **Best options for a specific need** — narrowly defined buyer-intent pages rather than unsupported universal rankings.
3. **Earn/save opportunities** — clearly separated from product recommendations and labelled by commercial relationship.
4. **Explainers** — educational pages that help users understand a category, offer, reward or purchase decision before the CTA.

## Programme selection principle

Commission rate is an internal commercial variable, not the primary consumer-facing ranking criterion. Consumer usefulness, merchant suitability, current terms, attribution reliability and evidence freshness take priority.

## Initial verified candidates

- EMU Australia — footwear/fashion.
- My Muscle Chef — meal delivery/food.
- Linen House — home/bedding.
- Windsor Smith — footwear/fashion, with publisher-approval caveat.
- The Somewhere Co. — work/baby/lifestyle.

SNDYS remains HOLD until its conflicting public commission information is resolved.

## Implementation gate

Before publication, every merchant page must have current programme eligibility, commission model, attribution window, consumer shipping/returns, source date and transparent affiliate disclosure. Pages must not claim "best", "highest paying" or similar without evidence supporting the comparative claim.
