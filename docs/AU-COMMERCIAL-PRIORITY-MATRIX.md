## Scoring definitions

- **User value:** usefulness, clarity and trust for an Australian visitor.
- **Commercial upside:** potential value of qualified clicks/conversions; not a forecast.
- **Content breadth:** number and quality of useful guides, comparisons and evergreen topics supported.
- **Technical readiness:** feed, deep links, stable destination structure and tracking practicality.
- **Risk:** policy, health claims, attribution complexity, eligibility or other restrictions. Lower is better.

## Launch gates

A program may move from research to a live commercial CTA only when:

1. publisher/referral relationship is confirmed;
2. current AU eligibility is verified;
3. current rate/reward and attribution terms are captured;
4. promotional restrictions are captured;
5. destination/deep link is tested;
6. disclosure treatment is defined;
7. click tracking and outbound resolution are available;
8. verification timestamp is stored.

## Strategic build order

### Phase A — Earn

Build the paid-research, surveys and user-testing comparison framework first. These categories have a clear user goal and can support comparison content without pretending that all platforms are equivalent.

### Phase B — Save

Build cashback and shopping categories next. ShopBack should be the flagship candidate, followed by selected retail programs with strong product data and meaningful attribution windows.

### Phase C — Compare

Add high-value telecommunications, appliance, technology and travel comparisons. These categories can produce higher-value commercial clicks but require stronger factual and offer-freshness controls.

### Phase D — Long tail

Only after the above works should the site expand into dozens or hundreds of merchants. Expansion should be feed/data driven rather than manually publishing thin merchant pages.

## Data policy

This matrix does not itself authorise publication. The canonical program record remains the structured seed/data layer. Editorial pages consume verified fields and must not become the authoritative source for volatile commission, reward or offer terms.
