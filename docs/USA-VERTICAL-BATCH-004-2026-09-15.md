## Record 1 — Respondent participant referral

**Evidence state:** CURRENT / VERIFIED PARTICIPANT REFERRAL.  
**Observed:** 2026-09-15.  
**First-party source:** Respondent Help Center, `Respondent Referral Policy for Participants`, published 2026-04-01.

Current first-party terms distinguish two participant-referral events:

- signup/network referral: **$20** after the referred new participant has earned at least **$75** from completed studies;
- project referral: **$50** after a new participant completes the specifically referred qualifying project; up to five paid referral bonuses per project.

Payment is described as a Tremendous virtual gift card after the underlying participant payment reaches PAID status. Self-referral and misleading/spam promotion are prohibited.

**Reconciliation:** the registry's prior statement that Respondent first-party referral figures were conflicting is now stale for the currently published participant-referral policy. The current policy supports the $20/$50 split above. This does **not** establish a publisher affiliate relationship.

**Publisher relationship:** UNKNOWN.  
**CTA implication:** participant-referral evidence may support editorial/member-referral classification only; monetised publisher CTA remains BLOCKED unless a separately approved relationship/destination exists.

Source: https://help.respondent.io/en/articles/5464425-respondent-referral-policy-for-participants

## Record 3 — Swagbucks member referral

**Evidence state:** CURRENT / VERIFIED MEMBER REFERRAL; publisher affiliate UNKNOWN.  
**Observed:** 2026-09-15.  
**First-party source:** Swagbucks Help Center.
