# UK Commercial Scoring Matrix — 2026-09-14

**Status:** Research prioritisation only  
**Country:** United Kingdom  
**Purpose:** Convert the broad UK candidate pool into launch, expansion and hold cohorts without confusing monetisation potential with publishability.

## Method

Each candidate is scored 0–5 on thirteen dimensions:

1. revenue potential;
2. purchase/lead intent;
3. AOV/CPA potential;
4. content/search depth;
5. catalogue breadth;
6. feed/API suitability;
7. UK fit;
8. trust;
9. programme stability;
10. restriction/validation resilience;
11. regulatory safety;
12. comparison suitability;
13. useful editorial depth.

The scores below are strategic prioritisation judgements derived from the current research register and should **not** be presented as measured conversion performance. Volatile programme terms require re-verification before publication.

## Decision rule

Do not promote a programme from research to production merely because it scores highly. Promotion requires the canonical publication gate:

`RESEARCHED → VERIFIED → PUBLISHABLE → PUBLISHED → MONITORED`

A candidate with excellent commercial potential can remain blocked indefinitely if programme approval, country eligibility, compliance, freshness or destination resolution is not proven.
