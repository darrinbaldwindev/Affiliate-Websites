## Priority interpretation

1. **Snooza** — strong combination of Australia focus, established pet-bed positioning, 7.5%+ commission and high AOV signal.
2. **Kurgo** — particularly useful for a differentiated dog travel/outdoor cluster; 8% commission and stated AUD $100–$150 AOV.
3. **Petzyo** — commercially attractive because of 15% new-customer commission, repeat-purchase model and product feed, but new-vs-returning economics must be modelled carefully.
4. **Pet Circle** — high consumer usefulness and broad catalogue; commission structure needs to be represented using the detailed new/existing rates rather than the directory headline alone.
5. **Collagenie** — attractive niche proposition, but health/wellness content requires stricter evidence and claims discipline.
6. **Jumbo Pets** — useful breadth and feed support, but lower commission than the leading candidates.

## Publication gate

Before any candidate is marked PUBLISHABLE, verify:

- publisher/account eligibility;
- current program terms and restrictions;
- tracking and attribution conditions;
- current shipping and returns;
- product availability for the intended Australian audience;
- consumer suitability and evidence for any substantive product claims;
- exact commission conditions, including new/returning customer rules and exclusions;
- freshness date and primary source.

## Important distinction

Commission Factory directory data establishes a current **program signal**, not guaranteed acceptance, conversion, earnings or customer demand. No revenue, conversion-rate or WTP claim is inferred from commission percentage.
