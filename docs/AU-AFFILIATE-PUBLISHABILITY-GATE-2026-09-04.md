# Affiliate-Websites — AU Affiliate Publishability Gate — 2026-09-04

## Purpose
Prevent commercially attractive affiliate programmes from being published as verified recommendations before publisher-level evidence exists.

## PUBLISHABLE requires

1. Publisher/account approval is evidenced for the actual site/account.
2. Current commission and qualifying-action terms are evidenced.
3. Cookie/attribution window and material exclusions are evidenced.
4. Australian customer/product availability is evidenced.
5. Product/category suitability for the intended consumer is evidenced.
6. Current landing/CTA path is tested or otherwise verified.
7. Source, checked date and evidence class are recorded.
8. No material disclosure, trust or compliance blocker remains.

## Statuses

- **RESEARCHED:** candidate identified; evidence incomplete.
- **VERIFIED SIGNAL:** primary source confirms programme/product information, but publisher gate remains incomplete.
- **PUBLISHABLE:** all required publisher-level and consumer-facing checks pass.
- **PUBLISHED:** live content has been released.
- **MONITORED:** live content is subject to freshness/terms monitoring.
- **HOLD:** a material evidence gap or conflict prevents publication.

## Commercial rule

Commission percentage is not an earnings forecast. A merchant's affiliate programme existing is not proof that this publisher has been accepted. Australian store availability is not automatically proof of Australian affiliate attribution.

## AI/agentic commerce rule

Do not claim a programme is AI-agent-ready merely because its products or affiliate links are available online. Technical transaction compatibility must be separately evidenced.
