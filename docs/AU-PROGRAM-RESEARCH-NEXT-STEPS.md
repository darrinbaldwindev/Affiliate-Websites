# AU Program Research — Next Steps

**Date:** 2026-09-03

## Current position

The AU research now has a seed dataset and commercial priority matrix. Commission Factory is the first network to establish because it exposes 600+ advertiser programs and provides program-level commission, tracking, validation and restriction information to affiliates. Current network documentation confirms advertiser profiles contain these fields and that the platform supports tracking/deep-link workflows.

## Immediate verification queue

1. Create/confirm the publisher account.
2. Apply to the highest-priority eligible programs.
3. Record acceptance/rejection and any account-specific rate.
4. Replace public-directory assumptions with account-specific terms where available.
5. Test a real deep link for each approved program.
6. Capture feed availability and update cadence.
7. Capture attribution restrictions and de-duplication rules.
8. Store verification timestamp and evidence source.

## Current research caveats

- Public network directory rates are not necessarily the final rate assigned to an individual publisher.
- Program pages can expose conflicting headline and detailed rates; the detailed/account-specific terms should win.
- Tracking windows, validation periods and promotional restrictions are volatile.
- Consumer referral programs require separate partner approval and must not be represented as publisher affiliate programs.
- A source URL used for evidence is not automatically the production outbound URL.

## New merchant candidates worth checking

The current Commission Factory directory also surfaces potentially useful AU candidates including Tefal, EMU Australia, The Daily Edited, Petzyo, The Somewhere Co., Anaconda and Global Shop Direct. These should enter the research queue where category fit is strong, but should not displace the initial launch set until approval, restrictions and commercial terms are verified.

## Decision rule

Prefer a smaller set of approved, verified programs with strong user intent and clean tracking over a large catalogue. The objective is qualified outbound traffic and useful consumer decisions, not maximum merchant count.
