# Affiliate-Websites — Australian Agentic Commerce Discoverability Evidence — 2026-09-04

## Purpose
Capture a current market signal relevant to the site's comparison, structured-data, trust and machine-discoverability strategy.

## Evidence
PwC Australia published **Agentic commerce explained: What Australian retailers need to know** on 17 August 2026. PwC says AI agents are beginning to research products, evaluate options and increasingly complete purchases for customers. It recommends retailers become discoverable, trustworthy, structured and transactable for an agent-driven environment.

## Classification

### VERIFIED
- Agentic commerce is an active Australian retail/consumer trend discussed by PwC in August 2026.
- PwC identifies structured, machine-readable information and trusted signals as important inputs for AI-agent product evaluation.
- PwC says Australian adoption is still at an early stage.

### INFERENCE / RECOMMENDATION
The Affiliate-Websites platform should treat high-quality structured comparison data as useful for both human readers and future machine/AI discovery. Existing fields such as verified status, reward/earnings, payout, method, effort, tracking, trust, source, freshness and reviewed date are therefore strategically useful beyond conventional SEO.

### UNKNOWN
- AI-agent traffic volume to the site.
- Which agents will drive material Australian affiliate traffic.
- Whether affiliate links/programmes will be directly usable by future shopping agents.
- Incremental conversion or revenue attributable to agentic discoverability.

## Guardrails
- Do not label a programme AI-agent-ready without technical evidence.
- Do not represent affiliate programme availability as publisher approval.
- Do not forecast affiliate revenue from agentic commerce adoption.
- Keep source, freshness and verification state visible.
- Agentic discoverability is a future distribution opportunity, not a substitute for consumer usefulness and verified affiliate terms.

## Source
PwC Australia, 17 August 2026: “Agentic commerce explained: What Australian retailers need to know.”
