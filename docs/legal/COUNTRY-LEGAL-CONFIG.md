# Country Legal Configuration

The master theme supplies structure only. Each country/site must provide approved legal configuration.

## AU

Configuration required from the Australia workstream:

- operating/legal entity
- approved contact details
- Australian privacy requirements applicable to the actual data processing
- consumer-law considerations
- affiliate disclosure requirements
- cookie/tracking and consent requirements applicable to the implementation
- approved governing-law wording if required

## UK

Configuration required from the UK workstream:

- operating/legal entity
- approved contact details
- applicable UK privacy/data protection requirements
- consumer-law considerations
- affiliate disclosure requirements
- cookie/tracking and consent requirements applicable to the implementation
- approved governing-law wording if required

## US

Configuration required from the US workstream:

- operating/legal entity
- approved contact details
- applicable federal/state privacy requirements
- consumer-law considerations
- affiliate disclosure requirements
- cookie/tracking and consent requirements applicable to the implementation
- approved governing-law wording if required

## Rule

Do not copy one country's final legal wording into another country merely because the page structure is shared. The **template is global; legal configuration is country-specific**.
