# Legal & Trust Page Register

## Master status
These are reusable page definitions for every country/site. Final wording must be approved for the jurisdiction and actual site configuration before publication.

| Key | Default path | Production gate |
|---|---|---|
| `affiliate_disclosure` | `/affiliate-disclosure/` | Legal review + relationship inventory |
| `privacy_policy` | `/privacy-policy/` | Legal review + actual data/processor inventory |
| `cookies_tracking` | `/cookies-tracking/` | Legal review + actual cookie/analytics inventory |
| `terms` | `/terms/` | Legal review + jurisdiction/configuration |
| `editorial_policy` | `/editorial-policy/` | Editorial owner approval |
| `corrections` | `/corrections/` | Editorial owner approval |
| `methodology` | `/methodology/` | Evidence/verification owner approval |
| `contact` | `/contact/` | Actual approved contact route configured |

## Rules

1. Keep the logical key stable across countries.
2. Country configuration controls destination URL, approved wording, legal identity and jurisdiction-specific requirements.
3. Never invent legal entity details, addresses, regulators, processors, cookies, retention periods or contact details.
4. Affiliate disclosures must be clear before or alongside commercial interaction, with a full disclosure page available.
5. Editorial recommendations and commercial relationships remain separate concepts.
6. Policy changes require a review/update event and an auditable publication record.
7. Placeholder content is not production-ready legal advice.
