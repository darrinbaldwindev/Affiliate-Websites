# Legal & Trust Page Register

Master template register. Country sites should map these logical keys to their approved local URLs/copy.

| Key | Default path | Required | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| affiliate_disclosure | `/affiliate-disclosure/` | Yes | Explain affiliate relationships and commercial transparency |
| privacy_policy | `/privacy-policy/` | Yes | Explain personal information handling |
| cookies_tracking | `/cookies-tracking/` | Yes | Explain cookies, analytics and tracking |
| terms | `/terms/` | Yes | Site terms and acceptable use |
| editorial_policy | `/editorial-policy/` | Yes | Editorial independence and recommendation standards |
| corrections | `/corrections/` | Yes | Error reporting and correction process |
| methodology | `/methodology/` | Yes | Research, evidence and verification methodology |
| contact | `/contact/` | Yes | Approved contact route |

## Configuration rule

The logical key is stable across the master framework; the destination URL, legal entity information and final wording are country/site configuration. Do not hard-code jurisdiction-specific claims into the master theme.

## Publication status

All pages are **TEMPLATE / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** until the applicable country workstream and appropriate legal reviewer approve final copy and the technical implementation has been verified.
