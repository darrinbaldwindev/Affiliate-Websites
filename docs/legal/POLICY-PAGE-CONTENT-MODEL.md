# Legal & Policy Page Content Model

**Status:** Master draft framework — legal review required before publication.

This document defines the reusable content structure for the legal/trust pages. It is intentionally free of fabricated business identity, address, jurisdiction, regulator, processor, cookie or registration details.

## Required configuration variables

- `[SITE_NAME]`
- `[LEGAL_ENTITY_NAME]`
- `[CONTACT_EMAIL]`
- `[POSTAL_ADDRESS]` if legally required
- `[COUNTRY]`
- `[GOVERNING_LAW]` where approved
- `[LAST_UPDATED]`
- `[PRIVACY_CONTACT]`
- `[COOKIE/CONSENT_PROVIDER]` if actually used
- `[ANALYTICS_PROVIDERS]` only after implementation inventory
- `[AFFILIATE_NETWORKS]` only after verified relationships exist

## 1. Affiliate Disclosure

### Purpose
Explain plainly that the site may earn commissions from qualifying commercial links.

### Required content
- what an affiliate link is
- that commissions may be received
- whether commission changes the price paid by the user
- that editorial standards are independent of commercial relationships
- how commercial relationships are identified
- statement that not every recommendation is monetised
- statement that availability, terms and commissions can change
- contact/correction route

### Inline disclosure
Use a short reusable disclosure near commercial CTAs, with a link to the full disclosure page.

## 2. Privacy Policy

### Required sections
- who operates the site
- information collected
- how information is collected
- purposes and lawful basis where applicable
- cookies and similar technologies
- analytics and advertising/measurement technologies actually used
- affiliate click/referral data actually processed
- sharing/disclosures
- international transfers where applicable
- retention
- security
- user rights
- complaints/contact route
- policy updates

No provider, cookie, processor or retention period should be named until confirmed in the implementation inventory.

## 3. Cookie & Tracking Notice

### Required sections
- essential technologies
- analytics technologies
- marketing/advertising technologies if used
- affiliate click tracking
- consent choices where required
- how to change/revoke choices
- browser/device controls
- actual cookie inventory maintained separately from this template

## 4. Terms of Use

### Required sections
- acceptance
- site purpose
- informational/editorial nature
- no guarantee of availability, pricing, rewards or outcomes
- external links and third-party terms
- intellectual property
- acceptable use
- limitation of liability, subject to applicable law
- changes
- governing law only after jurisdictional review
- contact

## 5. Editorial Policy

Explain:
- editorial independence
- research and sourcing
- commercial relationship handling
- recommendation criteria
- treatment of negative information
- updates and freshness
- author/editor responsibility
- conflicts of interest

## 6. Corrections Policy

Explain:
- how errors are identified
- how material errors are corrected
- update timestamps
- correction notes where appropriate
- how users can report an error
- distinction between corrections and ordinary content updates

## 7. Methodology / How We Verify

Explain the evidence lifecycle:

`CONCEPT → RESEARCHED → VERIFIED → PUBLISHABLE → PUBLISHED → MONITORED`

Use evidence states:

`FACT`, `VERIFIED`, `INFERENCE`, `RECOMMENDATION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `UNKNOWN`, `CLAIMED`.

For commercial data, document source, source type, verification date, verification status, confidence and freshness where applicable.

Conflicting information must be flagged rather than silently overwritten.

## 8. Contact

Provide approved contact channels only. Do not publish placeholder email addresses or invented physical addresses in production.

## Country review gate

AU, UK and US workstreams must provide jurisdiction-specific requirements and approved final wording. Legal copy is not production-ready until reviewed for the applicable entity, jurisdiction, privacy obligations, consumer law, affiliate disclosure requirements, cookies/consent and actual technical implementation.
