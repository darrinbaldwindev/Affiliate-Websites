How we research and verify

We aim to make recommendations using clear evidence, transparent criteria and current information. Commercial information is checked separately from editorial judgement.

  • We identify the information needed to make a useful decision.
  • We research primary and credible supporting sources.
  • We record verification status, source and date for material commercial claims.
  • We flag uncertainty or conflicting information instead of presenting it as settled fact.
  • We review important information as it becomes stale or changes.
