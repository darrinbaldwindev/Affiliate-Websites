What we compare

Eligibility

Rewards

Cash, gift cards, points or other reward types, with cashout conditions shown only when current evidence supports them.

Trust

Primary-source evidence, research-industry credentials, payment history signals and clear terms.

How to choose

  1. Check that the platform currently accepts Australian participants.
  2. Choose reward types you can actually use.
  3. Review cashout rules and any inactivity conditions.
  4. Expect screening and variable invitation volume.
  5. Prefer clear primary-source terms over unsupported earning claims.
